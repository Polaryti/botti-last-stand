# Changelog

## Development — Archipelagos of the Forgotten Gods

*After Port Vael, a ship sails on to Fontanova, the first island of an archipelago whose people worshipped gods now forgotten. One of them never left.*

### Fontanova, a new map
- **An island in the open sea**, for Survive the Night, Co-op, All vs All and Teams. The night lasts 15 minutes.
- You land in a cove beside the old merchant ship that brought you, run aground on the beach.
- Olive terraces climb from the shore; the Sacred Way's stair climbs on to the temple on the Acropolis crag, 34 m up.
- The worshippers' village stands on the rim of the Ravine. Two rope bridges cross it to the West Ridge and Kerys' Watch. A fall into the Ravine kills.
- The Holm Wood on its height, the Fallen Colossus, and the shattered statues of five gods.
- **Dense Mediterranean flora:** ten kinds of tree, a carpet of grass, and drifts of flowers and shrubs, all swaying in the wind.
- Fireflies, falling petals and leaves, shooting stars, and its own music.
- **The sea:** wading slows you, and out of your depth it drowns you after 2.5 s.

### Rites of Fontanova
- **The Oracle's Grotto:** light its three braziers to open the sanctum, which holds a rare weapon and the Oracle's Eye.
- **Oracle's Eye:** marks every enemy within 36 m, through rock, and your blows land 20% harder.
- **Waking altars:** from 2:30, one god's altar wakes every 2 minutes. Hold it for 10 s to earn that god's blessing for a minute.
- **The rising sea:** at 4:00, 8:00 and 12:00 the sea floods the beaches for a minute.
- **Transmutation blooms:** from 3:20, violet blooms break through somewhere on the island. Once open, they poison and hurt whoever stands among them.
- **Royal Jelly:** a broken Beehive also leaves Royal Jelly. It restores your health, mana and every ability, and quickens you for 45 s.

### Creatures of Fontanova
- **Mutant Bee:** curls its stinger forward, then dives where you stood. Its sting poisons for 4 s.
- **Beehive:** six grow each night. Every blow sends out 2 bees; broken, it spills 4 Honey.
- **Mutant Wasp:** shoots its stinger from afar (poisons for 3 s), or dives to bite up close.
- **Mutant Snail:** leaves a trail of poison or embers that lasts 8 s.
- **Wasp-Hive Snail:** its nest shudders, and a wasp crawls out, up to 3 at once.
- **Mutant Spider:** from 3:20, waits head down on tree trunks, then drops within 14 m. Its web holds and poisons.
- **Battle Ostrich:** paws the ground, then charges and kicks you away.
- **Ember Moth:** spreads its wings for 1 s, then claps; the dust dazzles and burns.
- **Thornback:** a great tortoise; blows from the front glance off for half.
- **Honey:** heals 25 and quickens you for 6 s, so it is worth eating unhurt.
- Every creature has its own model, sounds, icon, Codex page and death.
- The wasp's stinger is a venomous dart of its own, not an arrow.

### Title screen
- New app icon and splash. The menu tours Fontanova to the Archipelagos' own theme.

### Fixes
- **Codex:** when an enemy first appears is only shown once you have killed one.
- **Codex:** the Kraken and the Leviathan wear the boss crown, and bosses and minibosses no longer read "Appears from 166:39".
- **Whaler's Harpoon:** throwing it leaves its slot empty until it comes back. With both hands full by then, it lands at your feet.

## 0.22.1 — The Sunken Crown (24 November 2026)

*Down the road from the Valleys lies Vael, a harbour kingdom drowned when the Mists rose like a sea. Its Hollow Sovereign still holds court.* A new map with its own tide, creatures, court and bosses, a new class, coast weapons, nine Class Upgrades and the Trappers' Guild.

### Port Vael, a new map
- **The drowned harbour of Vael**, for Survive the Night, Co-op, All vs All and Teams. The night here lasts 15 minutes.
- The upper town and Market Square, the Harbourmaster's house, the chapel, the quay with cranes and warehouses, two breakwaters, a lighthouse, three wrecks and a drowned bell tower.
- The Boatwright's Yard, Ropewalk, Fishmarket and Salt House; coast paths under the firs to Gullwatch and Pilgrims' Garden.
- Marked racks at Gullwatch, Valley Road, Pilgrims' Garden and the Harbourmaster's House hold weapons and potions.
- Stairs, slipways and breakwater landings link the flats to the town. The Drowned climb out by them at any tide.
- A coastal aurora, sea mist, drowned spires on the horizon, and its own music and sounds.

### The Mist Tide
- **The tide turns every 90 s**, taking 12 s to come in or go out. The harbour bells ring 10 s before each turn.
- **Low tide:** each wreck and the drowned bell tower keeps a sea chest you can open once every low tide.
- **High tide:** the flats flood. Wading slows you, down to 60% chest-deep, the sea-mist half blinds you in the water, and the Drowned are a quarter quicker.

### The drowned of Vael
- **Drowned Sailor:** a cutlass, a boarding hook or a knife; sometimes leaves it when it falls.
- **Barnacle Brute:** its crust takes half of every blow from in front. Get round it.
- **Brine Hound:** hunts in packs of three, and lunges.
- **Lantern Jelly:** drifts in on the high tide and stings everything within 2.4 m, stunning for 0.8 s.
- **Angler:** lies buried with only its lure showing, and bursts out to bite.
- **Siren:** while she sings, everyone within 16 m is drawn toward her. A stun, or a fifth of her health lost, breaks the song.
- **Press Gang:** comes in threes and throws nets. Caught, you can't move for 1.6 s.
- **Tide Witch:** sends 3 waves at you in a line. At high tide she raises 2 Drowned Sailors.

### Above Port Vael
- **Wreck Gulls** spit salt. Their throats swell before the throw; step out of the splash.
- **Storm Rays** cup their fins and charge, then three lightning strikes cross a marked line. Dodge sideways.
- **Bell Wraiths** toll a knell that strikes a ring; its centre is safe. A stun stops the bell.
- At most three hunt at once, and roofs break their aim.

### The drowned court
- **Harbourmaster** (around 3:00): his hook drags you to him. Below half health his bell calls a Press Gang.
- **Kraken's Arms** (the high tide after 4:30): four arms rise from the flooded flats and slam down, sinking when the tide goes out.
- **Mad Lighthouse Keeper** (around 6:00): throws burning lamps. When he lifts his great lamp, turn your back or be dazzled for 1.5 s.

### Bosses
- **Hollow Sovereign** (around 8:30): waves in a line, the tide called in with his court rising round him, and below half health the crown's judgement.
- **Leviathan of the Mists** (around 11:30): its head strikes down, tidal waves roll out, and it dives, rising again with Drowned Sailors.
- **The Kraken**, the end of it all: once both have fallen, the harbour goes still and the Kraken rises in front of the quay. Dawn waits until it sinks.
- Its arms rear over each of you and whip down. It sweeps away anyone close, hooks whoever stands furthest off and spits blinding ink. At each third of its health it turns the harbour into a whirlpool.

### The Sunken Crown
- It falls with the Hollow Sovereign. Wear it to hit 25% harder and heal 2 health a second, but everyone can see you, and it falls when you do.
- In All vs All it washes up at the Drowned Bell at 2:00.

### A new class: the Tidecaller
- **Earned by winning a match in Port Vael**: reach the dawn there, or win a versus match there.
- 110 health. Spends tide, which its Trident draws, 6 with every blow that lands. Call the Tide rolls a wave 14 m ahead.
- **The Trident at the core of the tree:** Long Haft (reach, and one more in the line), Skewer (every third thrust strikes twice as hard) and Brine.
- **Tidewarden:** Tide Shell soaks up harm, refills with every Trident blow and bursts outward. Up to Unbroken Sea.
- **Stormcaller:** Trident blows soak enemies; Stormspear's lightning leaps between the soaked. Up to Maelstrom.
- **Deepcaller:** Call of the Deep raises a sea serpent that fights beside you and that enemies turn on. Up to Leviathan's Child.

### Weapons of the coast
- **Cutlass:** each kill quickens your swings by 15%, up to three times.
- **Boarding Hook:** drags what it catches 1.6 m towards you and staggers it.
- **Whaler's Harpoon:** pins for 2 s. Walk over it to take it back, or it returns after 15 s.
- **Flare Pistol:** sets alight everything within 3 m and shows every enemy within 7 m for 8 s, hidden ones too.
- **Ship's Anchor:** a slow two-handed slam; everything within 4.5 m takes half the blow and staggers.
- **Ferryman's Oar:** a wide two-handed sweep through up to eight, shoving them back.

### Class Upgrades for the Adept, the Cartographer and the Bandit
- **Encore** (Blade Dancer): every third stance change, a sweep that reaches further, hits 50% harder and knocks down.
- **Shattered Mirror** (Mirror Step): an undone hit sends 6 shards at the nearest enemies.
- **Event Horizon** (Void Walker): leaving Void opens a rift that draws enemies in, then falls in for 60.
- **Star Shower** (Star Reader): closing a constellation brings 5 stars down across it.
- **Open Door** (Worlds Traveler): a hit that would leave you under 30% is never taken; you step out of your farthest Waygate. Once a minute.
- **Trailblazer** (Pathfinder): friends on your trail move 20% faster; enemies on it are slowed.
- **Lucky Fingers** (Thief), **Contract** (Mercenary) and **Toll Road** (Highwayman) for the Bandit.

### The Trappers' Guild, for the Valleys
- A Map Upgrade for the Valleys of Mesenlla, up to Level 4: snare lines, Lodge hunters on the camp's towers, a hunters' cache every 60 s, and a fallback camp at the Ring of Stones.

### Screens
- **Armory:** a class you don't own shows how to unlock it, with a button to the Store where it is sold.
- **Store:** buying no longer sends the list back to the top. **Settings:** changing difficulty or Graphics keeps your place.
- **Codex:** an enemy's description stays hidden until your first kill. Sandbox kills don't count.
- New icons for the Bear, Mist Bear, Mist Eagle, Mist and Storm Elementals, Woodland Cavalry and the Daemon familiar.

### Creatures
- **Woodland Cavalry** ride a full harnessed bear that walks and gallops. Brought down, it rolls over and its rider fights on.
- Children of the Woods carry spears, bows and crossbows about 1 in 4 times (was 1 in 2).
- Bosses and minibosses shrug off crowd control and forced movement; damage still applies.

### Mist Bastion
- Gate archers hold the Long Bow or the Crossbow, at their proper size.
- Mist Soldiers outside a fallen gate always make it back to the portal.
- The invisible walls in the dry hollows are gone, and the battlements no longer set off auto-jump.

### Fixes
- Animations no longer play twice as fast on some creatures.
- Chest weapons drop beside the chest instead of swapping your own.
- Freezing a Child of the Woods turns all of it to ice.
- Online matches need everyone on 0.22.1.

## 0.22.0 — Beyond the Mists (16 November 2026)

*Having conquered the heart of the Mists, new paths open through its portals to distant lands.* Two new places, the creatures that live there, two new classes, 21 new Class Upgrades, Map Upgrades, Spectator Mode and a new look for every screen.

### Two new places beyond the Mists
- **A world atlas.** Survive the Night and Defend the Fort open a map of the world: tap a place to read its story, then Play here. A painted loading screen names the place, its story and a hint.
- **Valleys of Mesenlla** is a new map for Survive the Night, alone, together or in versus. A deep forested valley with a makeshift outpost, the Great Falls, a river with a log bridge and fords, mossy rock arches, the Ring of Stones and about 420 trees, giant firs among them.
- **The south is worth the walk.** The Fallen Hall, the Hunters' Lodge and the Old Mine each keep a cache of rarer weapons or a potion, refilled 100 s after it is taken. Three shrines bless you for a while: the Stag (25% faster for 30 s), the Bear (25% harder blows for 30 s) and the Mossy Spring (20 health, then 4 a second for 15 s).
- **The outpost falls.** From 2:00 the camp is shelled. At 5:00 everyone is told to leave; at 5:15 a barrage brings the watchtowers down and a Mist Storm settles over the camp for the rest of the night.
- **Mist Bastion** (Defend the Fort): a citadel on a mountaintop guarding a Great Portal. The creatures of Mesenlla climb three lanes to its west, middle and east gates, up to 1.6 times as many as on other maps. Mend the gates with wood; posterns let only players through.
- **The Mist Soldiers** step out of each lane's waystone from 0:20 and hold a post before their gate: Mist Infantry (117 health), Mist Rangers (72) and, from 3:00, Mist Heavies (221). When their gate falls they fall back to the portal.
- Each place has its own music for the night, the hunt and the dawn, and its own sound when the dawn is near. The title screen now tours the Valleys at twilight to a new theme.
- **The Mists:** a new Mist Storm gathers 12 s after the last one ends (the first at 0:45), and from 5:00 two can rage at once. No rain or storms there; about one night in ten beyond the Mists is windy.
- **The Map** draws each land in its own colours, with floating islands, rifts, gates by their state and the portal's health.

### Creatures of Mesenlla
- **Children of the Woods** carry whatever they found and drop something one time in five: their weapon, arrows or food. The **Woodland Cavalry** ride them in on bears.
- **Bears in fur.** The Bear rears up roaring before its maul; the Mist Bear rakes the ground and charges. Both have shaggy coats and new walks, gallops and blows.
- **Eagles and Mist Eagles** soar, hang screeching, then stoop with their wings folded and talons forward. The Mist Eagle's stoop leaves a ring of mist that bursts and slows.
- **The Shapeshifter** is a white stag until you come close, then a clawed beast, a Mist Wolf, a Great Owl or a Tusked Boar, a different shape every 12 s. Badly hurt, it flees as the stag: catch it within 10 s.
- **Earth, Mist and Storm Elementals** pound the ground, fade through walls and call lightning.
- **Wyverns of Fire, Ice and Storm:** each breathes its own element and bursts it where it dives. They fly with folding wings, glides and banking turns.
- Every special move has a tell and a ground circle in its own colour. Every creature has its own voice, its own death and a Codex entry.

### Bosses
- **The Great Revenant Elemental** rises at the Ring of Stones at about 8:30, a giant of boulders bound by mist. Dawn waits until it falls.
- **The War Wyvern** comes for Mist Bastion's portal at about 8:30, burning a lane every 22 s. Twice a wyvern's size, in barding and a war saddle, it carries two riders you can shoot out of the saddle.
- **Every boss speaks** as it enters, on a card of its own with its name, colour and bells, and as it falls. The Codex shows their words.
- Bosses no longer leave an Angel, the Veil Regent included.

### New classes
- **Bandit** (860 Doubloons): 110 health, 110% speed, a Dagger, a Long Bow and 20 arrows. Into the Shadows turns you invisible for 4 s. Each path adds a second ability: the Thief's Steal, the Mercenary's Nightfall dome or the Highwayman's Dagger Fan.
- **Cartographer** (earned: win 3 matches in the Valleys and 3 at Mist Bastion): 100 health, 105% speed and a Spear. Plant beacons linked by lines of light that speed and mend friends and mark enemies. Star Reader holds constellations, Worlds Traveler steps through Waygates, Pathfinder unearths finds.
- **The Adept** is back in the Store for 1,150 Doubloons.

### 21 new Class Upgrades
- Every path of these seven classes now has a Class Upgrade in the Store, each with its own scene played by the class itself. Bots use them too.
- **Chivalry:** Lance Charge, Consecration, Wild Hunt (replaces Whistle; owners of Whistle have it).
- **Magician:** Grave Call, Frost Nova, Hall of Mirrors.
- **Revenant:** Mist Wake, Torn Rifts, Soul Link.
- **Monk:** Dragon Spin, Fighting Body, Avalanche.
- **Plague Doctor:** Healing Mist, Plague Censer, Rat Tide.
- **Artificer:** Great Keg, Field Turret, Tesla Coil.
- **Commander:** Arcane Surge, Grapeshot, Shield Wall.

### Weapons and finds
- **Two-Handed weapons:** the Bell Hammer, Pitchfork, Scythe and the new Greatsword, which cuts through up to five. Infantry, Chivalry, Monk, Adept, Artificer and Commander can wield them.
- **Found only beyond the Mists:** the Battleaxe, whose third blow cleaves, and the Rift Chakram, which ricochets. Also the Stone Sprite familiar (a ward of stones that blocks a hit), the Mistberry Draught (hide and heal in your trail of mist) and the Antler Charm (your hits root enemies).
- Weapons found on one map only turn up a little more often there.
- After invisibility ends you are Revealed for 3 s before you can vanish again.

### Store and Armory
- **Map Upgrades:** Mist Reinforcements (2,200 Doubloons) strengthens the Mist Soldiers and puts Mist Rangers on Mist Bastion's gates, with a Long Bow for the lane and a Crossbow for the foot of the gate. Each player who brings it adds a level, up to Level 4.
- **Cosmetics:** the White Stag (Chivalry, 400 Doubloons). Try any look on in the Armory's new Appearance tab before you buy it.
- What you own leaves the Store and lives in the Armory, with On/Off switches for Map Upgrades. Upgrades for classes you don't own show as locked.
- The end of a match shows the leaderboard points and Doubloons it earned. Leaderboard rows show each player's class emblem.
- A new redeem code pays 2,000 Doubloons.

### Spectator Mode and playing together
- **Spectator Mode:** watch a match under way like a broadcast, from Matches to watch or with a private match's code. The camera follows the action by itself, or tap a player to stay on them. Up to 4 can watch.
- Play Together has two tabs, Same Wi-Fi and Online. The room shows each player's class emblem, and you pick your class from tiles.
- Bots play the new classes and upgrades, explore the new maps and no longer get stuck at the edge of a map.
- Online matches need everyone on 0.22.0.

### Seeing it coming
- **Special attacks show before they happen.** Marks under the crosshair count up to them, and the fire button and your weapon glow when one is next.
- **Numbers:** damage floats up from your blows (switchable in Settings), poison, bleeding and fire count down in their colours, and healing a friend shows green numbers.

### Screens, look and sound
- **Every screen redesigned** the same way: Back and the title at the top, tabs under the bar, real switches in Settings and buttons that give under your finger.
- **A cleaner HUD:** health, level and mana on one panel, status chips under the clock, your weapon by the attack button.
- **The Changelog** lists versions down the side, with each part in a card marked by its colour and icon.
- **A richer picture:** filmic colour, soft bloom on Medium and High, deeper shading, waterfalls of falling ropes and mist drifting through the trees.
- **Auto graphics** (the default on a new install) picks the right level for your phone and steps down if a match runs slowly.
- **Smoother play:** ground checks, lights, mist and glowing details cost far less, so busy fights run steadier.
- A new app icon and boot splash for Beyond the Mists.

### Balance
- Commander: Last Command every 35 s (was 60 s). The Warlock no longer loses 30 health and its War Staff hits harder.
- The Magician can pick up one-handed melee weapons beside the Staff.
- Walking backwards goes at 80% speed. Rain and storms are a little rarer.

### Fixes
- Arrows, darts and thrown weapons no longer hit through walls, and blows no longer land through windows.
- No more jumping on the spot at campfires, the river's edge or the edge of the map, and nobody can climb onto campfires or tents.
- Slows and bleeding from shots now affect rival players too.
- A win at the Fort no longer counts towards the Mists wins that earn the Revenant.
- The Changelog and Play Together open at once, and the game checks for a new version each time a match ends.

## 0.21.4 — A Portal Worth Defending (11 November 2026)

### The Fort
- The last bastion now protects a glowing portal to The Mists instead of the Heart. Defend the portal and keep the gates standing until dawn.
- Gate repair signs now say Wood. The Fort objective and Adept stance display no longer overlap.

### Your class and equipment
- The Armory introduces each class and its base stats, explains its three professions, and shows its Class Upgrades and equipment controls. Toggling an upgrade keeps your place in the list and your model preview.
- Class and profession introductions are more consistent. Beastmaster skill descriptions now refer to your pet and its ability.
- Commander has a new officer helmet and redesigned class and profession emblems, with matching frames.
- Monk's hands are visible again, with working punch animations. Magician can wield only the Staff. Attacking breaks invisibility.
- Plague Doctor now has 100 base health and 100% movement speed. Commander moves at 105% speed, and Artificer has 100 base health. Heavy Infantry starts with a Flail.

### Finds worth travelling for
- Scythes, Pitchforks, Throwing Axes and Torches are found in The Village and The Fort. Artificer still starts with a Torch on every map.
- Bell Hammers are found in The Mists. Their Codex badge uses the same blue as the Mists creatures.
- Plague Rat and Bell Spirit familiars, Carrion Crows and Dread Riders appear only in The Village and The Fort.
- Idle Clever enemies search farther for weapons they can reach.

### Clearer battles and field notes
- Fire and poison arrows have distinct effects that preserve their arrow shape, separate from fireballs.
- Invisibility adds a screen tint. Timed status icons and more distinctive combat messages make effects, backstabs and dodges easier to read.
- The Codex groups enemies into families, highlights map-exclusive entries, explains general status conditions, and introduces the three maps with their own emblems and lore.
- Removed duplicate descriptions and class prices from the Codex. The Armory explains professions and Class Upgrades without listing the entire skill tree.
- Menus respect mobile screen cutouts and safe edges. Touch lists no longer display scrollbars. Inspecting another skill clears the previous inspection ring.

### Fixes and playing together
- Fixed phantom fire, poison, blood and attack effects when starting a match.
- Temporary hit and freeze appearances now restore correctly when effects end or a player falls and respawns.
- Online matches require everyone to update to 0.21.4 so class balance and map rules agree.

## 0.21.3 — Steadier Online Games and a Fairer Fort (10 November 2026)

### Online games
- A player whose connection was slower than the others no longer falls further and further behind. Through the online relay, positions, poses and the clock used to queue up for a slow phone; now a late update is skipped and the newest one is used, so that phone stays in step and its timer agrees with the host's.
- Inventor sentries and burning or poisoned ground placed by another player now always appear on every phone; they could go missing before.
- Phones on 0.21.3 only play with other phones on 0.21.3: update every phone before playing together.

### Playing together
- The list of players shows everyone's health. A player who is down is greyed out with "Down, back in 14 s", and "Out" when they have no lives left.
- Buffs from another player, like a Commander's rally, appear in the row of effects with their time left, as do your own skills' buffs, a Shield Dome over you and a Guardian's cover while you stand near one.

### Controls
- The drop-weapon button has moved to the top row, beside the skill tree, and needs to be held for half a second ("Hold to drop"), so a passing thumb no longer throws a weapon away.

### Defend the Fort
- Enemies are sent evenly against all four gates and come from that gate's side. When a gate breaks, only its own attackers and about a quarter of the others go for it, so the other gates stay under pressure.
- Much more wood: 5 pieces for one player and 2 more per extra player, a new piece every 4 seconds while short. Almost half lies 6 to 15 m outside the walls, the rest out in the field or in the woods, never in a gateway.
- The wood a gate needs is painted on the wall above the gateway, inside and out, so it can be read from any angle. Gate names stand clear of the battlements.

### Classes
- The Adept is switched off for now. It can't be picked, bought or used by bots. Phones that own it keep it for when it returns, and its code is not used up.
- Commander: Warlock's Last Command also calls a Magician, and Gunner's Last Command an Artificer. Vanguard's Command calls an Infantry, and Last Command two Infantry and a Ranger, a new ally that shoots arrows.

## 0.21.2 — Leaderboard Prizes and Fairer Trees (10 November 2026)

### Leaderboard
- Each kind of match has its own board as well as the overall one: Night, Mists, Fort, Co-op and Versus (All vs All and Teams), each with all-time and weekly tabs.
- A new **Your matches** tab lists your last 30 counted matches: result, length, mode and map, class and profession, and points.
- The end screen says where a match left you, for example "+60 leaderboard points: #2 this week and #3 of all time (up 2)".
- Weekly prizes: when a week ends, the top three of that week's board win 120, 80 and 50 Doubloons (equal points share a place). The game hands them out the next time you open the menu online, once.
- Rude names are refused with "That name isn't allowed". Accents, numbers standing in for letters and stretched letters don't get round it; ordinary names that happen to contain a rude word stay allowed.
- Defend the Fort matches now reach the leaderboard. The 0.21.1 board turned them away.
- An admin page for the board (behind a private key) can rename or hide a player, reset their scores, remove them, or take back a single match. A renamed player's phone takes the new name.

### Skill trees
- Eight picks that only made zombies see or hear you less are gone; zombies already chasing you ignored them. In their place: Still Mind (Spirit Walker) slows enemies around you every 10 s; Void Appetite (Void Walker) stacks melee damage on kills; Bird Mask (Plague Doctor) heals 1 per second; Thick Mist (Revenant) also refills Mist 15% faster; Mist Landing (Mist Leaper) heals and returns Mist on landing; Rift Feast (Rift Traveller) heals on each hop; Cutthroat and Shadowstep (Shadow Blade) heal or speed you up on backstabs; Fletcher (Trapper) returns an arrow per kill.

### Balance
- Infantry has 110 health and heals 2 from every kill. Shadow Blade gains +20% melee damage and a 15% chance to avoid hits instead of quieter steps.
- Warpriest: Blessing heals 0.5 more per second instead of 1, and Shield of Faith comes every 50 s instead of 35.
- Paladin heals 0.5 per second on foot as well as while riding (1 per second riding). Horse Archer gets +20% ranged damage at all times and 2 arrows back per kill. The Mechanic's War Chariot mends 2 health a second.
- Blade Dancer sweeps heal 2 per enemy hit, up to 10. Opening Void stance heals 15. Mist Leap landings deal 20 damage around you and heal 5.

### Playing together
- Phones on 0.21.2 only play with other phones on 0.21.2: update every phone before playing together.

## 0.21.1 — Names and the Leaderboard (10 November 2026)

### Your name
- Choose a display name when you start the game. Other players see it in games together and on the leaderboard, and each name belongs to one player. Tap your name at the top of the menu, or in Play Together, to change it.
- Choose a name offline and it waits for the next connection. If someone takes it in the meantime, the game asks you for another.

### Leaderboard
- A new Leaderboard on the menu lists the top 50 players of all time and of this week (from Monday 00:00 UTC), with their points and their most-played class and profession. Your own place shows even when you're not in the top 50.
- Every finished match outside the Sandbox earns points: 1 per kill, 5 per full minute, 50 for winning. Easy scores 0.75 times as much and Hard 1.25 times. The end screen shows what the match was worth.
- Matches played offline are kept and sent later, and each counts once. The dev app and the released game have separate leaderboards.

### Classes and skills
- Monk now costs 920 Doubloons and Artificer costs 598. Adept is available in the Store for 1,150; existing owners keep their unlocks, and its class code still works.
- Guardian gains Oathkeeper: blocks store up to 20 healing, spent when raising Shield Dome to mend nearby allies and summons.
- Warpriest gains Last Rites: every third kill sends out a funeral flame, healing allies near the fallen and igniting surrounding enemies.
- Warlord gains Faultline: every third connecting swing splits a narrow line through the crowd, damaging, pushing and slowing enemies. Walls stop the fissure. Earthbreaker now counts connecting swings rather than each enemy struck.

### Store and Armory
- Heavy Infantry upgrades are now permanent Store purchases: Oathkeeper (240 Doubloons), Last Rites (260) and Faultline (280). Equip or disable them in the Armory; they work on their matching professions. The original free skill-tree choices return.
- Upgrade displays demonstrate their effects: a shower of arrows, a Berserker rising through flames, moving pets, flurries, shrouds, funeral flames, healing motes and ground fissures.

### Music
- The menu has a theme of its own. The Mists and Defend the Fort each have new music: one for the quiet, one for when you're hunted, and one for dawn.

### The Village
- Dry hillside navigation restrictions no longer form invisible walls for players. Deep-water and map-boundary barriers remain.
- The Windmill has collision matching its tapered tower. Bonfires have level ground beneath their full footprints, and the fires near Windmill Hill and Castle Road have clearer positions.
- Ground continues behind the boundary forest, covering the visible seam. A small memorial resting place fills the clearing north of the Church.

### The Mists
