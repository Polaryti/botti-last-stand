# Changelog

Newest first. The game shows this file on its Changelog screen, so keep the same layout: `## ` for a version, `### ` for a part of it, and `- ` for one change.

## 0.8.0 (3 November 2026)
### New
- The game is now called Botti Last Stand, with a new look: clearer lettering, dark panels and firelit colours in place of parchment and iron.
- New class, the Artificer: one weapon at a time, starts with a Torch. Three paths follow.
- Pyromaniac: throws incendiary bombs that leave the ground burning. Its last ability, Chaotic, makes every bomb throw off bomblets with random effects.
- Mechanic: kills build a war chariot. It is slower than a horse, takes part of each hit, and has room for a friend. The tree adds a turret, scythed wheels and more.
- Inventor: Invention gives a different device each time, and its button shows which comes next. Electric Volley, Cryo Flask, Clockwork Sentry, Steam Boots, Noise Box, Lodestone.
- The Store is open: buy the Revenant for 650 Botti Doubloons and the Artificer for 520.
- Sandbox replaces Roam: a small map with every weapon, item and familiar on show. Bring in any enemy, gain levels, and try any class you own.
- This Changelog screen.
- Maps can now be edited in the Godot editor: move, turn, copy or delete anything, and drag the hills, the river and the roads.
### Changed
- Three seconds of invincibility when you gain a level.
- The bell now strikes once for each minute gone, half a second apart, and can be heard across the whole map.
- The river carries you along if you wade in it, and water slows you down. The ford is now ankle deep.
- The village is laid out more naturally: roads bend, houses turn to face them, and nothing stands in the way. Two fields that used to lie across the roads have moved.
- The game turns over with the phone when you flip it, even with auto-rotate off.
- The screen no longer dims when a thumb covers the light sensor. Steady brightness and its level are in Settings.
- Locked classes now appear on the class screen with a padlock, and tapping through takes you to the Store.
- Codes can only be redeemed once. New code for the Artificer.
- The Codex now lists every class. Its tips page is gone, and the tips stay on the loading screen.
- New Botti Doubloon coin, and a new app icon: the village bell against the moon.
- The Torch has a real flame now, and it flickers.
### Fixed
- The Fort's stairs were hollow, so you could walk inside them and sink into the steps. They are solid now, and you walk on top.
- Two of the Fort's small doors opened straight into a staircase. They have moved to the other side of the gate.

## 0.7.0 (3 November 2026)
### New
- Defend the Fort: a second map with a walled fort, four wooden gates and a Heart to protect. Wood mends the gates.
- Guardian replaces Bastion: a Shield Dome, and taking hits for teammates.
- Dread Rider, a mounted enemy from minute 5.
- A Chivalry horse can carry a teammate, who can fight but not steer.
- Botti Doubloons, earned in every match. A locked Store button on the menu.
- Multiplayer: fallen players come back after 20 seconds and watch through another player's eyes. The host picks the difficulty.
### Changed
- The Summoner is weaker, and Mist Leap comes with a burst of fog.
- Bonfires, torches and lanterns reach less far.
- The Bell Hammer has a new look, a bell on a long shaft, and strikes from overhead.
### Fixed
- The joystick no longer sticks after you respawn.
- Names, halos and ghosts no longer disappear behind trees and the sky.
- Tapping pause more than once no longer breaks the menu.

## 0.6.0 (3 November 2026)
### New
- The Revenant, a hidden class: no weapons, claws of Mist, Mist Burst, and three paths.
- Redeem codes in Settings.
- Play through one phone's hotspot.
- Bonfires and torches light the map. Zombies see you further in firelight, and a bonfire heals you slowly.
- Map screen in the pause menu. Summons are shared across phones.
### Changed
- Crows warn before diving, take turns, and can be side-stepped and knocked down.
- Levels need a little more experience.

## 0.5.0 (3 November 2026)
### New
- Play Together: up to 4 phones on the same Wi-Fi, with Co-op Night, All vs All and Teams.
- A much larger map: real hills, a river with two bridges and a ford, a castle on a hill, a graveyard, mills, a farm, an orchard, hamlets and a lumber camp.
- Crops that sway and hide you: wheat, barley, corn and vegetable gardens.
- Keyframed attacks for every weapon.
### Fixed
- The music was almost all too low for a phone speaker to play. It has been rewritten.
- The mana and experience bars were hidden behind their frame.
- The arrow and text that gave away familiars are gone.

## 0.4.0 (3 November 2026)
### New
- A real ability tree for each class: a shared start, three branches, and a final ability near dawn.
- Mimic, Scarecrow, Wraith and Banshee. Icons for classes and abilities.
- Voice-like zombie sounds, and dark music that follows the night.
- A better sunrise.
### Changed
- Levels take longer, and familiars turn up more often.
- A new interface in iron and parchment.

## 0.3.0 (2 November 2026)
### New
- Five classes with their own abilities, chosen before each game.
- A church with a bell that rings each minute. More original weapons, familiars, and a horse to ride.
### Changed
- Zombies hunt more cleverly. Weapons disappear if nobody takes them.

## 0.2.0 (2 November 2026)
### New
- The game moves to a medieval village at night, with fog, buildings to enter and stealth.
- New weapons, items, potions and types of zombie.

## 0.1.0 (2 November 2026)
### New
- First version: first-person survival against zombies on Android, with weapons and items to find.
